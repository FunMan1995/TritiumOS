# HP Mini netbook

## Model 210-3070

Regular PC deployment works fine on it. Write it to a USB key, then you can boot
from it without problem.

The trackpad is a regular ps/2 mouse. Default PC initialization works. I haven't
yet figured how to configure the trackpad to, for example, disable the "click on
tap" feature, which is really annoying.

Video also works like on any PC. Both [drv/pc/vga] and [drv/pc/vesa] work.

But now you're running on INT13H [drv/pc/int13h]. If you want to do fun stuff
with EHCI [drv/usb/ehci], you'll likely fight the BIOS over it because it wants
to access the USB key.

You're better off installing Dusk on the internal hard drive, which is
accessible through [drv/pc/ahci]. Nothing special, it works as documented.

When you get EHCI working, you'll notice two internal USB devices. One of them
is the camera over the screen, the other one, named CRW, is the internal card
reader. It's a mass storage device, so it can be accessed with [drv/usb/blk].

Some PCI listings:

01:00.00 10ec/8136 Realtek RTL810xE PCI Express Fast Ethernet controller
02:00.00 1814/5390 Ralink  RT5390 Wireless 802.11n 1T/1R PCIe
