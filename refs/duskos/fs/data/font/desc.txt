# uxn fonts

Those fonts are of the UF1 [gr/font/uf1] and UF2 [gr/font/uf2] formats and come
from https://sr.ht/~rabbits/uxn/ or https://sr.ht/~rabbits/turye and are covered
under "licenses/uxn.txt" (MIT).

  atari8.uf1
  sans12.uf2
  term12.uf2
  chicag12.uf2
